02 / Research & innovation

Researching Explainable & Trustworthy AI

My research interests include Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI), Machine Learning, human-centric AI, model reliability, fairness, and the development of novel learning algorithms. I am especially interested in making complex model behaviour easier to interpret and communicate.

H

HHEX

Hybrid Human-Centric Explanation Algorithm. An approach I developed to explore more understandable and human-oriented explanations for machine-learning predictions.

S

SAFE-XAI

A research initiative focused on exploring stable, fair, reliable, and interpretable explanations for AI systems.

C

CESA-ML

A Machine Learning research and development initiative within my continuing exploration of intelligent learning approaches.

ML

Novel ML Algorithms

Designing and investigating new algorithmic approaches, studying their mathematical foundations, and evaluating their potential applications.

My XAI work also involves studying established techniques such as SHAP and LIME, understanding their strengths and limitations, and exploring ways to improve explanation quality.

05 / Education

Teaching Is My Passion

Teaching is much more than a profession to me; it is something I genuinely love. I find great satisfaction in explaining challenging concepts in simple language, helping students build confidence, and seeing them progress from uncertainty to understanding.

My teaching philosophy is grounded in conceptual clarity, patience, practical learning, and encouragement. I believe students learn best when they understand why a concept matters and have opportunities to apply it themselves.

  • Build strong fundamentals before moving to advanced topics.
  • Use examples, demonstrations, coding exercises, and real-world applications.
  • Encourage questions, curiosity, independent thinking, and creativity.
  • Connect mathematics and theory with practical programming and AI.
  • Create a supportive environment where every student can grow.
